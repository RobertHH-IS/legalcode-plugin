# Legalcode Legal Memorandum

Define jurisdiction, date, objective, issues, material facts, and assumptions. Use Discover to confirm coverage; Search to find authorities; Fetch to verify propositions and versions; and Trace for cited, citing, implementation, or legislative-history relationships. Research adverse authority and record coverage gaps.

Structure the memorandum as Question Presented, Short Answer, Material Facts and Assumptions, Applicable Law, IRAC or CREAC Analysis, Counterarguments and Adverse Authority, Conclusion, and Sources and Verification Notes.

Every material legal proposition needs a verified citation or an explicit uncertainty label. Preserve sourceRef, official URL, filters, and version details. Do not overstate predictive confidence or imply that aggregate patterns prove legal outcomes.
